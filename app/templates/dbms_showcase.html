3. PL/SQL Stored Procedures, Functions & Triggers

PROCEDURE complete_session(p_session_id)

Executes atomic credit debit from learner, credit add to mentor, double-entry transaction insert, session status change, attendance insert, and automated certificate issuance. Enforces full transaction commit or automatic rollback on failure.

FUNCTION calculate_match_score(p_req_id, p_mentor_id)

Weighted 0–100 matching formula: 40% proficiency tier match + 30% average star rating + 20% experience & sessions + 10% availability overlap.

TRIGGER trg_user_after_insert

After insert on Users, automatically provisions a wallet record in Credit_Accounts with 50 welcome credits, adds a bonus ledger transaction, and creates a welcome notification.

TRIGGER trg_check_session_integrity

Before insert/update on Sessions, validates that mentor and learner are distinct students, duration > 0, and credit cost >= 0.
